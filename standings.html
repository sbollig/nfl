Reading the numbers. W-L (or W-L-T when a game ended tied) is the overall record; Pct is winning percentage. Streak is the current run: W3 means three straight wins, L2 two straight losses. Home and Away split the record by venue. Div is the record against the other three teams in the same division, Conf against the same conference; both are tiebreakers. PF/PA are points scored and allowed; +/− is their difference. Seed is the current playoff seed (1–7 in each conference, blank if out). GB is games behind the division leader.
