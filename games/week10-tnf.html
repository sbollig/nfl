Where is Jayden Daniels? his 2026 numbers against the other 31 teams' leading passers, through Week 9

Answer: there are two quarterback questions, and Daniels is the better one. Against the other 31 teams' leading passers, Daniels is 11th in passer rating (97.2, median 91.2) and 1st in interception rate, with none in 180 attempts. He is second in QB rushing yards (252, behind Josh Allen's 285). The rest is middle of the pack or worse: 18th in completion rate (63.9%), 20th in yards per attempt (6.9), 21st in yards a game (208.3 over six games), 25th in touchdowns (7) and 25th in sack rate (8.2%).

The Giants' leading passer is not their starter. Jameis Winston (1,028 yds, 8 TD, 5 INT, 79.6 rating) leads them in passing yards, but J.J. McCarthy is listed first on ESPN's depth chart. McCarthy is 43 of 68 for 622 yards, 9.1 an attempt, 1 TD, 1 INT and a 91.7 rating, with 9 sacks. He took the Baun hit in Philadelphia, came back and practiced fully Tuesday (giants.com, Nov 10).

Reassessment Mack's pick against the market, and what would move it

Mack has Washington; the market has the Giants. Mack's line is Giants +1.9, so the Commanders are favored by about two. DraftKings has Giants −2.5 (moneyline Giants −142, Commanders +120). That is a 4.4-point gap, and it crosses zero. ESPN's Matchup Predictor sits in the middle: Giants 50.1%, Commanders 49.5%. Mack: Commanders 56%, Commanders on the spread, Over 41.5.

What would change it. If McCarthy is ruled out, Winston (79.6 rating, 5 INT) starts against a defense with 12 takeaways, which strengthens Mack's side. If Carter plays at full speed against a backup center, the Giants' rush is the strongest reason to side with the market. Burks being out would leave Washington's receivers thinner still.

Mack's view (judgment). The two teams tied 27-27 in Week 5, and both offenses score 21–23 a game. The Over at 41.5 rests on Washington's 25.3 points allowed and the Giants' 68% red-zone touchdown rate.
