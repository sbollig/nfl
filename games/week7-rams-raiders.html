The readMack's ratings make this a near coin flip (Rams 55%, fair line Raiders +1.7) while the market lays 4.5 with the Rams, and the week's news only widens that gap: Davante Adams, Poona Ford and kicker Blake Grupe are all on IR and Trent McDuffie is questionable. Stafford is still throwing for 299 yards a game, and the Rams run the ball far better (4.6 a carry to 3.0). But Las Vegas has Cousins's 14 touchdown passes, two tight ends with 56 catches, 15 sacks to the Rams' 8 and Matt Gay against the Rams' third kicker of the season. The pick is Rams straight up, Raiders with the points, and the Over.

Reassessment Mack's pick against DraftKings and ESPN's predictor

Mack's ratings and the market disagree by almost three points. Mack has the Rams winning 55% of the time with a fair line of Raiders +1.7; DraftKings has Rams −4.5 and ESPN's Matchup Predictor gives the Rams 66.7%. That makes the Raiders the spread side, and the Over the total side at 48.5.

What the week has added. The ratings were frozen before the Rams' injury wave: Adams, Ford and Grupe gone, Turner reported to IR, McDuffie questionable. Every one of those pushes toward the Raiders' side, not away from it.

What would change it. McDuffie clearing Friday and Garrett taking a full workload would narrow the gap; the line moving to Rams −3 or below takes most of the value out of Raiders +4.5. On the total, Raiders games have averaged 54.0 combined points and Rams games 45.5; the Over leans on Las Vegas.
