The readTampa Bay just won a game without completing a pass: 324 rushing yards and four interceptions of Michael Penix in a 16-0 downpour win over Atlanta (ESPN recap, Nov 1), and Baker Mayfield has thrown 14 passes in the two games since his dislocated thumb (buccaneers.com, Nov 4). Chicago has won three straight and beat the defending champion Seahawks 34-17 on Monday night with Caleb Williams at 24 of 34 for 247 yards and Jeffery Simmons, acquired Thursday for two first-round picks, getting a sack on his first drive (ESPN, Nov 2). The whole game is Bucky Irving and Kenny Gainwell against a Bears run defense that allows 86.8 yards a game but 4.4 a carry, and now may be without both starting defensive tackles next to Simmons. Mack's line is Bears −6.8 against a market of −6.0, with the total leaning Under at 46.5; the Bucs' 131 net passing yards a game and 12 interceptions on defense are why.

ESPN's injury feed for game 401873050 (fetched Nov 4) lists Yaya Diaby (OLB, knee/MCL) as OUT — Todd Bowles said he "won't be available for Sunday's game against the Bears" (ESPN injury page, Nov 2) — and Emeka Egbuka (WR, foot) as QUESTIONABLE. Chicago's side is the bigger story. ESPN lists four Bears as QUESTIONABLE: DT Grady Jarrett (groin, doubtful to return Monday night), DT Kentavius Street (knee, did not return Monday), CB Malik Muhammad II (toe, inactive Monday) and RB Kyle Monangai (toe, four straight games missed). Bears OnSI's Gene Chamberlain (Nov 3) wrote that Jarrett and Street getting hurt is why Jeffery Simmons played 55 of 63 snaps in his debut, and that rookies Jayden Loving and Jordan van den Berg are next up; he also relayed an unofficial report that LT Theo Benedet said "separated shoulder" in the locker room, which no official source confirms. Cole Kmet (TE), Dayo Odeyingbo (DE), Braxton Jones (OT) and Coby Bryant (S) are on IR.

Where is Caleb Williams? And where is Baker Mayfield? their 2026 numbers against the other 30 teams' leading passers, through Week 8

Answer: Williams is top ten in everything about accuracy and ball security and bottom third at staying upright; Mayfield is last in volume and 31st at staying upright. Against the 32 teams' leading passers by yards, Williams ranks 2nd in completion rate (70.9%), 6th in passer rating (104.6), tied 6th in yards per attempt (8.0) and 4th in interception rate (0.6%); his sack rate of 7.6% is tied 21st. Mayfield, in the same five games played, is 32nd in yards per game (135.8) and touchdowns (3), 25th in rating (80.8), 26th in yards per attempt (6.2), and his 11.3% sack rate is 31st, ahead of only Deshaun Watson. His ball security is fine: 6th-fewest interceptions, tied 11th in interception rate. Both missed three games, Williams with a hamstring strain (buccaneers.com, Nov 4) and Mayfield with a dislocated right thumb (Athlon via Yahoo, Nov 4).

Coaching staffs tenure, experience, and what the beat has said this season

Buccaneers

  • Todd Bowles, head coach since 2022 (Jets head coach 2015–18); Wikipedia's head-coaching table shows 38–38 in the regular season and 1–3 in the playoffs in Tampa Bay. He runs the defense himself; the team lists no defensive coordinator. After Sunday, asked if he had ever won without a completion: "I have not, but there is a first time for everything." (NFL.com, Nov 1)
  • Zac Robinson, offensive coordinator, hired Jan 22, 2026; Falcons OC 2024–25, Rams pass game coordinator and QB coach 2022–23. The Bucs' fifth offensive coordinator in five seasons.
  • Danny Smith, special teams coordinator; Chandler Whitmer coaches the quarterbacks.

Bears

  • Ben Johnson, head coach, hired Jan 21, 2025; 17–8 regular season, 1–1 playoffs (Lions OC 2022–24). He still calls the offensive plays (670 The Score, Feb 18). After Monday: "When the pressure's high, we want to be at our best and all three phases did that." (chicagobears.com, Larry Mayer, Nov 2)
  • Press Taylor, offensive coordinator, promoted from passing game coordinator in Feb 2026 after Declan Doyle left for Baltimore.
  • Dennis Allen, defensive coordinator, second season; Raiders head coach 2012–14 (8–28), Saints head coach 2022–24 (18–25).
  • Richard Hightower, special teams coordinator, fifth season in the role; J.T. Barrett coaches the quarterbacks; Al Harris is defensive backs coach and passing game coordinator.

Missed tackles Tampa Bay's number since Week 1, and Chicago's

Tampa Bay: 50 missed tackles, tied with the Eagles for fourth-most in the NFL; Chicago: 34, tied for 21st (Pro Football Reference, via BetMGM, Nov 4). Denver leads with 70; Detroit has the fewest at 15. On a per-game basis (nfllines.com, through Week 8) the Bears miss 3.38 a game, second-fewest, and the Bucs 6.25, 28th; the league average is 5.11.

The exceptions matter. Jacob Parrish had seven tackles, two run stuffs and no missed tackles against Atlanta (buccaneers.com, Nov 4). Chicago held Seattle to 252 yards and one third-down conversion Monday, and Simmons' 48 snaps held the Seahawks to 3.0 yards a play (ESPN, Nov 2).

Why it matters Sunday. Irving and Gainwell both average 5.0 a carry and just ran for 310 yards between them; a Bears defense that tackles as well as it has is the one thing that keeps this from becoming the Atlanta game again. The other way round, Swift is at 3.8 a carry and needs the Bucs to miss.

Reassessment Mack's line against the market, and what would move it

Where the board stands. Mack's line is Bears −6.8 with a 69% win probability, spread side Bears, total Under; the market, frozen at the board build, is Bears −6.0 and 46.5. ESPN's Matchup Predictor gives Chicago 62.9%, and ESPN's page showed the line at −6 with the total at 47.5 when fetched Nov 4, after an open of −3.5 / 48.5 (DraftKings via ESPN). The market moved toward Chicago after Monday night; Mack's rating gap was already there.

Under the formula a non-QB out costs −0.5, doubtful −0.3, questionable −0.15, capped at −2.5 a side; as listed that is −0.65 for the Bucs and −0.6 for the Bears, a wash.

What would change it. If Jarrett and Street are both ruled out, the Bears' interior against Irving is Simmons and two rookies, and the 0.8-point cushion over the market gets thin; I would not go past −6. If Egbuka plays and Mayfield throws like he did in September, the Under gets harder, but 131 passing yards a game and a defense leading the league in interception rate is a low-scoring profile on both sides. The Under is the stronger of the two calls.
