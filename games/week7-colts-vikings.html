The readMinnesota is 5-0 because of its defense: 64 points allowed in five games and 17 sacks. Indianapolis comes in at 2-4 after losing to Tennessee 26-20. Daniel Jones has 4 touchdown passes, 6 interceptions and a 71.1 rating, 30th of the 32 teams' leading passers. The Colts' best weapons are Jonathan Taylor (7 rushing TDs) and Spencer Shrader, who is 18 of 18. Mack has the Vikings winning (66%), but his −5.8 is shorter than the market's −6.5, so the spread pick is Colts +6.5, and he takes the Over at 41.5.

Wednesday is the first Week 7 practice day. ESPN's injury page (fetched Oct 21) lists Sauce Gardner (CB, mild left calf strain, week-to-week) as DOUBTFUL, which this page treats as out. It also lists Tyler Warren (TE, groin) as QUESTIONABLE. He was not seen in the open portion of practice on Oct 21. Jaylon Carlies (LB), Ashton Dulin (WR), Anthony Gould (WR, concussion) and punter Rigoberto Sanchez (foot) are questionable. Minnesota's side: ESPN says T.J. Hockenson (TE, concussion) and Jordan Addison (WR, hamstring) were expected to practice Wednesday, and both are QUESTIONABLE. Jordan Mason (RB, thumb) has been designated to return from IR. LT Christian Darrisaw is questionable; he missed Week 5 with a concussion (Football Nation, Oct 19). G Donovan Jackson and C Nick Samac are on IR.
