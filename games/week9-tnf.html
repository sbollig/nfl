The readBaltimore is down to its third quarterback: Lamar Jackson is headed to injured reserve after thumb surgery, Tyler Huntley's season is over with turf toe, and undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano makes his first NFL start on four days' rest against the league's second-ranked scoring defense (15.9 points a game). Jacksonville has outscored opponents 110–40 in first halves, leads the league in point differential at +85, and holds opposing runners to 3.4 yards a carry, which is the one thing Baltimore still does well (Derrick Henry: 158 carries, 632 yards). The Ravens have scored 10, 13 and 10 in a three-game losing streak and have been outscored 134–54 after halftime this season. Mack's ratings make the Jaguars a 71% winner but set the line at Ravens +7.6, so the board takes Baltimore against the 9.5 and the Over at 41.5; the market has since moved to Jaguars −10 and 39.5, and both moves are about Fagnano. Mack's view: the Ravens side rests on Henry and a defense that held Buffalo's offense to 5.6 yards a play, not on any evidence from the quarterback.

Baltimore starts Joe Fagnano. Jesse Minter announced it Tuesday: "We'll be starting Joe at quarterback this week on Thursday … He's cool, calm and collected, doesn't really get rattled" (ESPN, Jamison Hensley, Nov 3). Lamar Jackson hurt his right thumb on Baltimore's first offensive play in Buffalo, needs surgery and is headed to injured reserve, so he misses at least four games (baltimoreravens.com, Nov 1 and Nov 4). Tyler Huntley (toe) is listed OUT on ESPN's feed with a Feb 15 return date; tests showed season-ending turf toe (baltimoreravens.com citing Adam Schefter, Nov 4). Fagnano went 10 of 21 for 91 yards, one interception, three sacks and a lost fumble in relief Sunday. He took a low hit from Ed Oliver, wore a brace on his right ankle Tuesday and was still a full participant (ESPN, Nov 3). Kedon Slovis was signed off Green Bay's practice squad to the 53 as the backup; Austin Reed is on the practice squad. Fagnano is the second undrafted rookie quarterback to start for the franchise (Anthony Brown, 2022).

Baltimore's kicker is new too. Tyler Loop (right groin) went on injured reserve Tuesday after missing a 34-yarder in Buffalo, his fourth miss of the year (13 of 17); Jake Moody, elevated from the practice squad, kicks Thursday. Moody was 18 of 20 for the Bears and Commanders last season and is 64 of 82 for his career (ESPN, Nov 3; Ravens Wire via Yahoo, Nov 3). Minter: "When he's been here, he's kicked well in practice."

Ravens practice report (Monday/Tuesday, Tuesday's is the latest posted): DNP Calais Campbell (DE, rest), Jaylinn Hawkins (S, knee, QUESTIONABLE on ESPN), Jackson, Huntley. Limited John Jenkins (NT, knee), Roger Rosengarten (RT, ankle, QUESTIONABLE), John Simpson (LG, back, QUESTIONABLE). Full after being limited Monday: Roquan Smith (foot), Trey Hendrickson (finger/ribs), Ronnie Stanley (toe/ankle), Justice Hill (groin), Olaivavega Ioane (knee). Mark Andrews (hand) and Nate Wiggins (stinger) were full both days (jaguars.com injury report page and russellstreetreport.com, fetched Nov 4). Under the house rule, questionable is not out.

Jacksonville's side: four starters were hurt against the Colts. DT DaVon Hamilton (torn quadriceps) and S Eric Murray (elbow) are on IR. RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot) did not practice Monday or Tuesday; Liam Coen said Wednesday he is feeling better and that it will likely be a game-time decision (SI via RotoWire, Nov 4). ESPN lists him QUESTIONABLE. If he sits, Chris Rodriguez Jr. (45 carries, 209 yards, 3 TD; himself limited with a hand injury) is the lead back. DE Travon Walker (ribs) went from DNP to limited and is QUESTIONABLE; WR Jakobi Meyers (hamstring/ankle) was limited both days and is QUESTIONABLE. Limited but not on ESPN's questionable list: RG Ezra Cleveland (knee/ankle; Coen expects him to play, per Ravens Wire via Yahoo, Nov 4), S Antonio Johnson (ankle), CB Jarrian Jones (ankle), LB Branson Combs (shoulder).

This is the game. Jacksonville has 9 interceptions and 32 passes defended in seven games and is one of three teams with more interceptions than touchdown passes allowed (NFL.com, Dan Parr). Coen scouted him before the draft and likes him ("he's got some baller in him … great anticipation and he's accurate," per ESPN Radio's Mia O'Brien via the Times-Union, Nov 4), but when Jackson missed three starts earlier this year Baltimore scored 30, 10 and 13 with Huntley, a fifth-year veteran. Fagnano has 21 NFL attempts. Clearest edge on the page.

Oluokun's plan is "stopping the run first." The edge is real but it is the matchup the Ravens have to win to cover.

Where is Trevor Lawrence? his 2026 numbers against the other 31 teams' leading passers, through Week 8; and what Joe Fagnano brings

Answer: the most efficient scorer in the league, with ordinary volume and ordinary ball security. Among the 32 teams' leading passers Lawrence is 1st in touchdown rate (8.6%), 2nd in passer rating (112.6, behind only Lamar Jackson's 118.4 in five games), 3rd in yards per attempt (8.5) and 3rd in touchdown passes (17, behind Burrow's 20 and Cousins' 19). He is 15th in passing yards a game because Jacksonville has not needed the fourth quarter: he averages a league-low 5.1 fourth-quarter dropbacks (Sharp Football). His interception rate (2.0%) and sack rate (6.2%) sit at the median. Fagnano is not among the 32: his line is 10 of 21, 91 yards, 0 TD, 1 INT, a 40.0 rating, with 44 college starts at Maine and UConn behind it (48 TD, 6 INT in three UConn seasons, per NFL.com).

Coaching staffs tenure, experience, and what the beat has said this season

Jaguars

  • Liam Coen, head coach, hired Jan 24, 2025; 13–4 and an AFC South title in his first season, then a 27–24 wild-card loss to Buffalo; 5–2 this year. Rams offensive coordinator in 2022, Tampa Bay's in 2024. On the short week: "You're really not trying to throw new at the guys," and on the injury pile from Sunday, "you got four starters get hurt in one game" (Florida Times-Union, Demetrius Harvey, Nov 4).
  • Grant Udinski, offensive coordinator, hired Feb 5, 2025 at 29 from Minnesota (assistant OC); extended Jan 27, 2026 after Cleveland interviewed him for its head job.
  • Anthony Campanile, defensive coordinator, hired Jan 30, 2025; linebackers coach with the Dolphins 2020–23 and Green Bay 2024.
  • Heath Farwell, associate head coach and special teams coordinator; Spencer Whipple coaches the quarterbacks.

Ravens

  • Jesse Minter, first-year head coach, announced Jan 22, 2026 as the franchise's fourth, succeeding John Harbaugh; 4–4. Chargers defensive coordinator 2024–25, Michigan DC 2022–23, Ravens defensive backs coach in 2020. On Fagnano: "An opportunity we're fired up about" (NFL.com, Nov 3).
  • Declan Doyle, offensive coordinator, hired Feb 2, 2026 at 29; Bears OC under Ben Johnson in 2025, Broncos tight ends coach 2023–24. He now game-plans for a third quarterback in eight games.
  • Anthony Weaver, defensive coordinator.
  • Anthony Levine Sr., special teams coordinator; Israel Woolfork coaches the quarterbacks, including the one making his first start.

Missed tackles Jacksonville's number since Week 1, and Baltimore's

Jacksonville: 4.57 missed tackles a game, 11th-fewest; Baltimore: 6.00 a game, 23rd (nfllines.com advanced-defense table, through Week 8, fetched Nov 4; league average 5.11). Detroit is best at 2.14, Cleveland worst at 7.75. Over the season that is roughly 32 misses for the Jaguars and 48 for the Ravens.

Why Baltimore's number is the one that matters. Tuten ranks 5th among backs in missed tackles forced (43) and 5th in yards after contact per rush (3.9), and he forced six misses against the Colts for 79 yards after contact (Yahoo Sports Jaguars Week 8 notes, citing PFF). A Ravens defense missing six tackles a game against that back, or against Rodriguez if Tuten sits, is how a 39.5 total goes over.

Jacksonville's tackling is the Henry test. Henry gets 0.87 yards before contact per rush (33rd among backs, per Sharp), so he is being hit early and the question is whether he stays up. The Jaguars have given up 3.4 a carry all year and 1.5 and 1.2 a carry in their last two home games. Tackle leaders: Oluokun 51, Miller 41, Johnson 30 for Jacksonville; Smith 66, Hamilton 57, Starks 51, Simpson 51 for Baltimore (ESPN box scores summed).

Reassessment Mack's frozen pick against a market that has moved a point toward Jacksonville and two points under

Mack and the market agree on the winner and disagree on the price. The ratings make Jacksonville a 71% winner but put the line at Ravens +7.6, two points short of the board's 9.5, so the board side is Baltimore and the total side is Over 41.5. Since the build the DraftKings line at ESPN has moved to Jaguars −10 and the total down to 39.5 (Nov 4); ESPN's Matchup Predictor gives Jacksonville 81.0%. The Ravens are home underdogs by 10, their largest in 11 years (ESPN, Nov 3). Both moves are the market pricing an undrafted rookie at quarterback, which a power rating built on eight Baltimore games, five of them with Jackson, does not fully see.

What would change it. The pick is frozen, but the inputs are not. Under the formula a quarterback out is the big input, and Baltimore has two of them out plus a kicker on injured reserve; Hawkins, Rosengarten and Simpson at questionable are −0.15 each. On Jacksonville's side Hamilton and Murray are gone (−0.5 each), Tuten, Walker and Meyers questionable. If Tuten is ruled out Thursday morning the Over loses its best argument. If Fagnano's first drive looks like his half in Buffalo (4.3 an attempt, three sacks), the Ravens side is a bet on Henry and a defense that has allowed 35 and 36 in two of the last three.

Mack's view (judgment, not the formula). The 9.5 was already a lot of points for a Jacksonville team that is 1-1 on the road; 10 is more. But the Over at 41.5 was set before the market learned Fagnano would start, and 39.5 is where I would have set it myself. Baltimore has scored 33 points in three games. I would keep the Ravens side and hold my nose on the Over.
