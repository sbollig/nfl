The readKansas City is 5-0 and the market still has the Chiefs as road underdogs; Mack's line has them winning by 1.6, a 4.1-point edge against Seahawks −2.5, the biggest reason this game is on the page. The engine is a former Seahawk: Kenneth Walker III, Super Bowl MVP for Seattle in February, has 665 rushing yards at 6.3 a carry for the Chiefs, behind a line that has allowed 6 sacks. The case for Seattle is its front, 16 sacks and 37 tackles for loss, against a Chiefs left side with Josh Simmons questionable. The case against Seattle is its quarterback: Darnold's 4.7% interception rate is the worst of the league's 32 leading passers, and the Chiefs have 9 takeaways to Seattle's 12 giveaways. Ryota saw this Seattle team up close in Denver on Oct 15; it has since gone to 3-3.

Missed tackles Walker against his old team

The one tackling number that matters here belongs to the runner. Through three games Kenneth Walker III had forced 35 missed tackles, "the most in the NFL by eight, according to PFF," and Next Gen Stats had him as the only player with 10 or more in a single game, which he had done three times (A to Z Sports, Sept. 28). He has run for 128 yards and two touchdowns on 19 carries since, against the Chargers (ESPN, Oct 18).

Seattle's tacklers. Linebacker Ernest Jones IV leads Seattle with 59 tackles and nickel Nick Emmanwori has 23; if Drake Thomas (35 tackles) cannot go, Tyrice Knight takes his snaps. Seattle allows its opponents' backs nothing like 6.3 a carry, but no Seattle opponent has had Walker.
