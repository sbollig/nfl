The readMack takes the Browns to win (54%) and the Raiders with the points, because Cleveland wins close: all six of its victories came by four points or fewer. Fernando Mendoza makes his third start after 11 sacks in his first two, and he meets a defense with 27 sacks and 67 tackles for loss. Cleveland's own protection is no better, with 42 sacks allowed in 10 games, and its offense scores 18.8 a game. Las Vegas allows 29.7 points and 149.5 rushing yards a game, and three of its defensive tackles were hurt in Denver. If Brock Bowers returns, the Raiders have the best receiver in the game; if not, the case for the underdog is thinner.

No official Week 12 report was readable when this was written. NFL.com's Week 12 page showed no entries for either team on Wednesday Nov 25, and the first official report is due that day. ESPN lists Brock Bowers (TE, ankle) as QUESTIONABLE. He was hurt against Seattle, missed the Wednesday practice of Week 11, was limited Thursday and Friday and sat out Denver with ankle and quad injuries. Klint Kubiak said he "was not ready to play" but was optimistic about Cleveland: "He's made progress" (Yahoo Sports).

The middle of the Raiders' line: Adam Butler (concussion), Tim Keenan III (knee) and JJ Pegues (forearm) were all hurt in Denver, where Tonka Hemingway (stinger) was inactive. ESPN lists all four as QUESTIONABLE. Kubiak had no update Monday on the three hurt in the game (AP, Nov 23). WR Dareke Young (stinger) is also questionable. TE Michael Mayer left the Denver game with a back injury and returned in the fourth quarter (Yahoo Sports, Heavy).

Cleveland's side: WR Denzel Boston (toe) is QUESTIONABLE and did not practice Tuesday. RG Austin Barber, DEs Isaiah McGuire and Derek Barnett and LB Quincy Williams also missed that unofficial practice (BrownsZone, Nov 24). S Ronnie Hickman (concussion) is questionable on ESPN but returned to practice Nov 18, and SI expects him back. RB Raheim Sanders (hip) had his return window opened Tuesday; FB Michael Burton (calf) is on IR.

Where are Mendoza and Watson? their 2026 numbers against the 27 passers with at least 200 attempts, through Week 11

Answer: Watson is a middle-of-the-pack passer who takes far too many sacks; Mendoza is too new to rank. Watson is 17th of 27 in rating, 12th in yards per attempt and 6th in interception rate, but 26th in touchdown rate and 26th in sack rate. Only Justin Herbert is sacked more often. He does add 266 rushing yards, fourth among this group. He missed Week 9 with a concussion and returned against Houston.

Mendoza has 74 attempts and is shown unranked. His completion rate (55.4%) would be the lowest in the group and his sack rate (12.9%) the highest. His 11 sacks in two starts are the most by a Raiders quarterback over two games since 1982 (AP, Nov 23). Kirk Cousins, traded to Minnesota Nov 10, threw 20 of the Raiders' 23 touchdown passes, so the season passing totals describe a different offense.

Missed tackles Cleveland is tied for the league's most; Las Vegas is mid-table

Cleveland: 79 missed tackles, tied with Denver for the most in the NFL; Las Vegas: 51, tied with Green Bay and San Francisco for 16th most (Pro Football Reference, via BetMGM, article dated Nov 25). The Lions have the fewest at 30. The page does not state its cutoff.

The yardage runs the other way. Las Vegas allows 149.5 rushing yards a game at 4.8 a carry to Cleveland's 126.3 at 4.6, and has given up more than 100 on the ground in every game since the opener (AP, Nov 23).

Why it matters Sunday. Jeanty has 430 yards after the catch. If Cleveland misses tackles on checkdowns, Mendoza has an answer to the rush that does not require protection to hold up.
