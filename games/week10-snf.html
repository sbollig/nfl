The readThis is a matchup of two 6-2 teams, and the Bengals' passing game is the difference: Joe Burrow leads every team's leading passer in completion rate (74.0%), yards per game (298.2) and touchdowns (23), and he is throwing to Tee Higgins (758 yards) and Ja'Marr Chase (53 catches, 7 TD). Pittsburgh's case is its run game and its defense. Jaylen Warren is averaging 5.9 yards a carry, Cincinnati runs for only 3.9, and Pittsburgh has allowed 18.0 points a game to Cincinnati's 21.9. The Steelers won the Week 3 meeting 30-27, but Aaron Rodgers has been sacked 23 times, and Boye Mafe and Dexter Lawrence II have 10.5 sacks between them. Mack has the Bengals by 4.7 and the Under; the market sits a point closer at −3.5.

Reassessment how Mack's pick and the market compare, and what would move it

Mack: Bengals 64%, Bengals −4.7, Under. Market: Bengals −3.5, total 48.5 at the board build. Mack's line is 1.2 points stronger on Cincinnati than the market's, which is why the spread side is the Bengals. ESPN's Matchup Predictor is even more bullish, giving Cincinnati 70.4% (fetched Nov 11).

The total. ESPN's feed showed 49.5 on Nov 11, a point above the frozen 48.5. That move goes against Mack's Under. The case for the Under is Pittsburgh's defense (18.0 points allowed a game) and its run game shortening the clock. The case against it is Cincinnati: 30.0 points a game, with 35 and 43 scored in two of its last three games.

What would change it. Pittman and Harmon coming off IR would help Pittsburgh at both lines of scrimmage. Both corners, Slaughter and Echols, being out would push further toward Cincinnati. A Dugger absence matters less, given Cincinnati's safety depth with Battle and Cook. The Steelers won the first meeting 30-27, and that score went over this total.
