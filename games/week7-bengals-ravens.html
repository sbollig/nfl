The readThis game turns on Lamar Jackson's ankle. With him, Baltimore has the league's top-rated passer and a 151.5-yard-a-game run game behind Derrick Henry. Without him, the Ravens scored 10 at Cleveland on Sunday. Cincinnati comes off its bye with Burrow completing 71.4%, a 50% third-down rate and 17 sacks in five games, so it is the more complete team on paper if Huntley starts. The market has already moved from Ravens −3 to Bengals −1.5. Mack's Ravens pick needs Jackson on the field Sunday.

Lamar Jackson (QB, ankle) is listed QUESTIONABLE on ESPN's injury feed (updated Oct 21) with a return date of Oct 25. He was hurt on a sack at the end of the first half of the Week 4 win over Tennessee and has not started since. Jesse Minter said Wednesday afternoon: "Lamar was actually able to get a little bit of limited work in, in preparation … it's a day by day thing" (SI, Jason La Canfora, Oct 21). It was his first time back on a field since the injury. Tyler Huntley started at Atlanta (24/32, 201, 2 TD, a 30–23 overtime win) and at Cleveland (19/31, 165, 1 TD, a 13–10 loss), per ESPN box scores. I have not seen the official Wednesday practice report yet, so Friday's game status is the number to watch.

More Baltimore questionables (ESPN): Rashod Bateman (WR, shoulder), Cam Jurgens (C, concussion), Andrew Vorhees (G, foot), Broderick Washington Jr. (DT, ankle). Under the house rule, questionable is not out.

Cincinnati's side: Kyle Dugger (S, quadriceps) and Colbie Young (WR, knee) are questionable. Tanner Hudson (TE, pectoral), Swayze Bozeman (LB, forearm) and Jalen Davis (CB, hamstring) are on IR.

Where is Joe Burrow? his 2026 numbers against the other 31 teams' leading passers, through Week 6

Answer: Burrow is top ten in almost every column, and Jackson leads the league when he plays. Among the 32 teams' leading passers, Burrow ranks 6th in passer rating (104.4), 2nd in completion rate (71.4%), 7th in yards a game and 7th in sack rate. The one ordinary number is 16th in yards per attempt (7.2): he has been efficient, not explosive. Jackson ranks 1st in rating (119.6) and yards per attempt (10.1) in four games. Baltimore's real starter for Sunday may be Tyler Huntley, though, who is outside the 32 (99.8 rating, 6.0 a pass, 0 INT on 72 attempts).

Missed tackles who makes the tackles for each side; missed-tackle counts pending

Missed-tackle counts for these two teams were not published on a page I opened this week, so this card stays on what the box scores show.

Tackle leaders. Baltimore: Roquan Smith 47, Kyle Hamilton 46, Trenton Simpson 41, Malaki Starks 38. Cincinnati: Demetrius Knight Jr. 42, Barrett Carter 41, Jordan Battle 32. Both teams' second level leads in tackles, which fits the matchup: Henry runs at Knight and Carter, and Cincinnati's short passing game runs at Smith and Simpson.

Why it matters Sunday. Henry is at 4.1 a carry and needs volume. Cincinnati's run defense has to tackle him on first contact for 60 minutes, which is the one way a Huntley-led Baltimore offense gets beaten.
