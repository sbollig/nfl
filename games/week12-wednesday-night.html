Is Rams −5.3 too rich?

Mack's answer: yes, by about two points. The pick stays with the Rams. The size of the edge does not hold up.

RamsPackers

Injury charge before the cap4.0 points1.7 points
Injury charge used in the line2.5 points (capped)1.7 points

How the line is built. Ratings alone make it Rams −6.1. Subtract the Rams' 2.5-point injury charge, add back Green Bay's 1.7, and the result is Rams −5.3.

Where it falls short. Every non-quarterback who is out costs the same half point, up to 2.5 per team. Puka Nacua costs the Rams exactly what a third-string center does, and once a team reaches the cap the next loss costs nothing. Without the cap the same arithmetic gives Rams −3.8.

The rating underneath. Mack's main index still carries some preseason expectation and has the Rams at +7.5, third in the league. On this season's results alone they are +4.2, tenth. Green Bay is +2.7 either way.

Mack's read. Put those together and a fair line is close to Rams −3: still the Rams to win, with little to spare against a market of −2.5. This is Mack's judgment on top of the model, not a model output, and the board pick stays as it was frozen on Tuesday.

The preview's case

  • The injury gap in one line: "Green Bay has all its key offensive weapons at its disposal. The Rams do not."
  • On the Rams' 34 points without Nacua and Adams: impressive, "but it was versus the Cardinals whose defense is terrible." Stafford completed passes to 10 different receivers in that game.

Where Mack and the panel differ. The analysts weigh the Rams' missing receivers and Parsons' return more heavily than the betting line does. Mack weighs the run game, third down and the red zone. Both readings point to a one-score game.

Final injury reports are in (packers.com, published Nov 24, 12:00 PM). Green Bay ruled nobody out and listed eight players QUESTIONABLE: Micah Parsons (EDGE, knee), Josh Jacobs (RB, finger), Zaire Franklin (LB, shin), Sean Rhyan (C/G, ankle), Kevin Zeitler (G, triceps), Travis Glover (G/T, shoulder), Bo Melton (WR, thigh) and Skyy Moore (WR, ankle). All eight were limited on Sunday, Monday and Tuesday except Glover, who had no listing Sunday; Tuesday was a walkthrough, so that day is an estimate. Aaron Banks (knee), Brandon Cisse (rib) and Isaiah McDuffie (rib) were full on Tuesday and carry no status.

Parsons. He tore his ACL and meniscus last December against Denver and returned to practice Nov 4; his 21-day window closes Wednesday (packers.com). SI's final-report piece (Nov 24) says the Packers activated him and that he is expected to make his season debut. He has no 2026 statistics on ESPN. Jacobs had surgery on a broken ring finger suffered in Week 9 and missed the Minnesota game. LaFleur on him playing: "I'm not anticipating that, but we'll see how he progresses," and "He would definitely have to prove the function in order to play." (SI, Nov 24). SI also says Franklin is expected back after missing the last four games and that Rhyan, who missed the last three, may return at center.

Rams. Steve Avila (LG, hamstring), Coleman Shelton (C, hamstring) and Terrance Ferguson (TE, ankle/hamstring) are OUT; none of the three practiced. Puka Nacua (knee) and Davante Adams (hamstring) are on IR. therams.com's injury page (fetched Nov 24) says Nacua needs MCL surgery and is out a "couple months," that Shelton's hamstring strain is a 4-6 week injury (Nov 17), and that Ferguson stays on IR for the rest of the season (Nov 23). Kobie Turner (DE, groin) and Justin Dedich (G, hand) were full all three days with no status; ESPN's feed still showed both on injured reserve with a Nov 25 return date when fetched. Myles Garrett (knee/rest), Kyren Williams (toe), Kam Curl (toe) and Matthew Stafford (right elbow, full all week) have no status. The Rams did not practice Tuesday, so that day is an estimate.

Missed tackles both teams mid-table; the Rams' run-first plan makes it Green Bay's problem

Green Bay: 51 missed tackles, 14th-most in the NFL; Rams: 48, 19th-most (Pro Football Reference, via BetMGM, published Nov 18; the article does not state a week cutoff). Cleveland has the most at 79, Detroit the fewest at 25. Neither defense in this game is at an extreme.

Why it still matters Wednesday. The Rams' offense is now built on the run and the short catch: 288 rushes for 1,330 yards, and 1,275 of their 2,636 receiving yards after the catch (ESPN). Against Arizona they ran 46 times, and the AP recap noted Xavier Smith "broke a few tackles" on his 22-yard touchdown. Green Bay's leading tackler is a safety, Evan Williams with 84, which says how often ball carriers reach the second level.

The other direction. Green Bay's backs average 3.1 a carry as a team, so the Rams' tackling is tested mostly by Watson and Golden after the catch (Green Bay has 1,160 yards after the catch on 233 receptions).
