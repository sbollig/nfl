The pick in one line49ers −3, O/U 46.5. Purdy 9.0 yds/att vs. a MIN D allowing 14.0. Vikings questionable: LB Andrew Van Ginkel +1. Mack: 49ers win and cover, Under.
The readMinnesota's defense has to carry this game. It has 37 sacks, 17 takeaways and 14.0 points allowed a game, and it is facing the league's most efficient passer: Brock Purdy at 9.0 yards an attempt and a 115.2 rating, with an offense scoring 31.2 a game and converting 82.8% of red-zone trips. Kirk Cousins makes his second start since returning to Minnesota. Three weeks ago, as a Raider, he threw three interceptions against this same 49ers defense. The Vikings' clearest edges are their pass rush and their kicking game (Reichard 23 of 25, Pineiro 8 of 11). Mack has San Francisco by 5.7 against a market of 3 and takes the Under.

Kyler Murray's hamstring and Kirk Cousins's return. Minnesota got Cousins back from Las Vegas at the trade deadline for a 2027 third-round pick. He started at Green Bay in Week 10 and won 30-10 (21/31, 207 yds, 2 TD, 1 INT per ESPN). ESPN's depth chart lists Kirk Cousins, Kyler Murray, Carson Wentz, in that order.

Vikings (ESPN injury feed, Nov 17): Jordan Addison (WR, thumb) OUT; Andrew Van Ginkel (LB, quad), Blake Cashman (LB, concussion), Jauan Jennings (WR, calf) and Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins (DE, ankle) QUESTIONABLE. Hockenson (chest/wrist) missed Week 10 and is "expected to progress."

49ers (ESPN injury feed, Nov 16–18): Brandon Aiyuk (WR, personal) OUT; Mike Evans (WR, Achilles tendinitis, missed three straight) and Renardo Green (CB, back) QUESTIONABLE; Nate Hobbs (CB, groin) and C.J. If they play, this is the one place Minnesota can take the game over. If either sits, the edge shifts.

Where are Cousins and Purdy? their 2026 numbers against the other teams' leading passers, through Week 10

Answer: Purdy is the most efficient passer in the league; Cousins has a 2026 season that is mostly a Raiders season. Against the 32 teams' leading passers, Purdy is 1st in yards per attempt (9.0), 2nd in rating (115.2, behind Joe Burrow), 3rd in completion rate and 3rd in touchdown rate. ESPN's season table credits Cousins's full 2026 line to Minnesota, so he is the Vikings' leading passer: 13th in rating, 2nd in touchdowns (22), 5th-best in sack rate, and 27th in interceptions (9). Three of those nine came against the 49ers in Week 9. Murray (100/154, 5 TD, 3 INT, 86.6 rating in six games) has a grade 2 hamstring strain, per NFL on Prime's James Palmer.

Missed tackles what can and cannot be confirmed for both teams

Missed-tackle counts through Week 10 were not available from a source opened for this page, so there is no number here. Rather than estimate, this section uses what ESPN's numbers do show.

What the box scores say. Minnesota's defense has 66 tackles for loss and allows 14.0 points a game. San Francisco gains 4.9 yards a carry and has 10 rushing touchdowns from McCaffrey. The 49ers' playmakers after the catch are Kittle (12.7 a catch), Samuel (12.5 a catch, 13.0 a carry) and Watkins (22.8 a catch on 12 receptions). If Minnesota tackles the first man, San Francisco's yards-per-attempt edge shrinks.

Linebacker availability is the tackling story. Cashman (76 tackles) is questionable with a concussion and Wilson has 57. The 49ers' Warner (88) and Greenlaw (65) are healthy.

Reassessment how Mack's pick and the market compare, and what would change it

Mack has the 49ers by 5.7, the market by 3. Mack's pick is the 49ers to win (66%) and to cover, and the Under. ESPN's Matchup Predictor has San Francisco at 63.1%. DraftKings via ESPN was 49ers −3.0, total 46.5 at the board build. ESPN's summary showed −3 and 45.5 on Nov 18, so the total has dropped a point toward Mack's Under.

What would change it. For Minnesota: Van Ginkel and Cashman both playing, and Green missing for San Francisco. That would put a 37-sack defense against a secondary down to Lenoir on a sore foot. For San Francisco: Evans returning to a passing game that already averages 9.0 yards an attempt. A Murray return would not change Sunday; SI reported he may miss another week anyway.

Mack's view (judgment, not the formula). The 2.7-point gap is about the right size for what these numbers show. San Francisco is far better on offense, and Minnesota's best units are its pass rush and its kicker. The Under rests on Minnesota's 47.6% red-zone touchdown rate and its defense allowing 14 a game. The risk is that the 49ers have scored 38 and 46 in their last two games.
