The readSan Francisco is 6-0 because Purdy is playing like the second-best quarterback in football (118.1 rating, 14 touchdowns) behind a line that has allowed five sacks. Atlanta's case is Bijan Robinson (641 yards at 5.8) and Drake London (550 yards), and a Penix who has been efficient in his four games. The 49ers arrive thin: Samuel out, Evans and Bosa uncertain, Stout and Sigle questionable in the secondary. Mack's ratings agree San Francisco wins (55%) but make it Falcons +1.8, so the lean is Atlanta with the points and the Under 50.5. ESPN's Matchup Predictor is far more bullish on the 49ers at 78.7%.

Where are Purdy and Penix? their 2026 numbers against the other 30 teams' leading passers, through Week 6

Answer: Purdy is second in the league; Penix, in his four games, is closer than his team's record says. Against the other 31 teams' leading passers, Purdy is 2nd in passer rating (118.1, behind Lamar Jackson's 119.6), tied for 1st in touchdown passes (14, with Kirk Cousins), 1st in touchdown rate, 4th in yards per attempt and 2nd-lowest in sack rate. Penix has played four games (Cooper Rush started the first two) and is 5th in rating, 3rd in yards per attempt (8.6, a hair above Purdy) and 5th-lowest in interception rate, but 21st in sack rate and 31st in rushing. The difference Sunday is volume and protection, not arm.

How they compare. Mack and the market pick the same winner; the gap is 1.2 points on the spread, enough for Atlanta at +3. ESPN's predictor is much further toward San Francisco than either.

What would change it. Evans and Bosa both playing pushes the number toward the market; Evans out with Samuel already out leaves Purdy with Kittle, Cooks and McCaffrey. On Atlanta's side, Daniels and Deablo both out thins the middle against McCaffrey. A Penix setback would flip the side: Rush has 4 interceptions in 39 attempts.

Mack's view (judgment). The Under is about two offenses that may be short their best receivers and run-heavy game scripts; 50.5 needs both teams near their averages (29.5 and 23.7 sum to 53.2), so this is a lean, not a lock.
