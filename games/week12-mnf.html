The readThe records say Carolina and the points say Tampa Bay: the 5-5 Panthers have been outscored 269–254, while the 3-7 Buccaneers have outscored opponents 223–209 and lost six of their seven by one score. Tampa Bay won the first meeting 43–13 in Charlotte with 258 rushing yards and four takeaways, and it still brings the league's top rushing offense (159.5 yards a game) against a defense allowing 127.3. The worry is the other matchup: Carolina throws for 250.6 net yards a game and Tampa Bay's cornerbacks are hurt, with Benjamin Morrison absent Wednesday and Jacob Parrish back at practice. Baker Mayfield is playing through plantar fasciitis, and Tampa Bay is last in net passing yards. Mack takes the Buccaneers to win (67%), the Buccaneers against the spread, and the Over.

Tampa Bay's corners are the story. Jacob Parrish (back) and Benjamin Morrison (hamstring) both left the Detroit game and had MRIs scheduled for Monday; no results had been published in the pages opened here. SI's Wednesday practice report had Parrish back at practice and Morrison absent, with no participation levels. ESPN's feed, fetched Nov 25, still lists both as QUESTIONABLE. Parrish leads the team with 14 passes defended. Kevin Knowles finished the Lions game at corner, Ayden Garnes was inactive, and Josh Hayes is on IR with his practice window open. Todd Bowles on the depth: "It hurts when you go down to your third corner." (buccaneers.com)

Also in Tampa Bay: S Antoine Winfield Jr. (ankle) was inactive at Detroit and is QUESTIONABLE; DT Elijah Simmons is in the concussion protocol and QUESTIONABLE. WR Jalen McMillan (knee) and LB Christian Rozeboom had their return windows opened; both remain on ESPN's IR list. Baker Mayfield (plantar fasciitis) played Sunday, was sacked four times, and has no ESPN injury designation this week.

Carolina's side: LB Bobby Okereke (foot) is QUESTIONABLE after missing the Ravens game. CB Jaycee Horn (quadriceps) and S Isaiah Simmons (knee) are on IR, RB Jonathon Brooks (groin) is on IR with a return designation, and G Brady Christensen is done for the season after his window lapsed Tuesday. T Ikem Ekwonu is listed OUT and T Taylor Moton is on IR. The good news: WR Jalen Coker cleared the concussion protocol (NBC Sports, Nov 23), and CB Mike Jackson returned against Baltimore.

Where is Bryce Young? his 2026 numbers, and Baker Mayfield's, against the league's leading passers through Week 11

Answer: the middle of the league as a passer, top ten in volume, and bottom quarter at staying upright. Among each team's leading passers, Young is 7th in yards per game and 17th in both passer rating and yards per attempt. He is 26th in sack rate, behind a line missing both tackles. Mayfield is 29th in rating and 31st in yards per game, in seven games and with a foot injury. The gap between them is real, and Week 7 showed how little it can matter: Mayfield threw 11 passes and won by 30.

Coaching staffs tenure, experience, and what the beat has said this season

Panthers

  • Dave Canales, head coach since Jan 25, 2024; 18–26 in the regular season with the 2025 NFC South title. He was Tampa Bay's offensive coordinator in 2023, when Mayfield threw for 4,044 yards and 28 touchdowns.
  • Brad Idzik, offensive coordinator since 2024 and the play-caller this season; Tampa Bay's receivers coach in 2023.

  • Tracy Smith, special teams coordinator.

Buccaneers

  • Todd Bowles, head coach since March 30, 2022; 38–40 in the regular season in Tampa with three division titles, 1–3 in the playoffs and 5–14 since a 6–2 start last year (AP). He runs the defense. On the schedule left: "We've got to win every ballgame." (panthers.com)
  • Zac Robinson, offensive coordinator and play-caller, hired Jan 22, 2026; Atlanta's coordinator in 2024–25.
  • George Edwards, pass game coordinator on defense.

Missed tackles Carolina's count from Sunday, and Tampa Bay's PFF grades

Carolina missed "13 to 15 tackles" against Baltimore, by Canales's own count (AP, Nov 23). The Ravens ran 22 times for 157 yards, 7.1 a carry. Carolina survived by taking the ball away four times, two on fumbles Will Lee III forced.

Tampa Bay's problems are at specific spots. PFF's Detroit grades (Yahoo, Nov 24) charged two missed tackles each to Al-Quadin Muhammad and Kevin Knowles, the corner who may have to play again. Josiah Trotter led with 14 tackles and an 83.8 tackling grade.

Why it matters Monday. PFF had Bucky Irving forcing five missed tackles as a runner at Detroit. Tampa Bay already ran for 258 on this defense once, and Carolina's tackling is the first thing Wash has to fix.

What supports it. Tampa Bay's point difference is +14 to Carolina's −15, its turnover margin is +7 to +3, and its best unit meets Carolina's worst. The Week 7 game is evidence, with limits: Carolina lost four turnovers that day and has changed defensive coordinators since.

What would change it. Mayfield's foot is the largest input; Daniels has three touchdowns and five interceptions. Next are Parrish and Winfield: if both miss along with Morrison, Young's matchup improves sharply. For Carolina, Okereke's status matters most against this run game.

Mack's view (judgment, not the formula). The total makes sense to me in the Over direction: Carolina allows 26.9 points a game and a touchdown on 75.9% of red-zone trips, and Tampa Bay's injuries are in coverage. The risk is a long, run-heavy night that shortens the game.
