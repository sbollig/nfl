The readHouston should win this game. The question is whether it wins by two touchdowns. C.J. Stroud has 19 touchdowns and 3 interceptions, and Will Anderson Jr. leads the NFL in sacks. Daniel Jones has a league-high 12 interceptions, and the Colts traded Keenan Allen and Josh Downs at the deadline. Indianapolis may also play without both starting tackles and both starting corners. Houston's own record argues against laying 12.5: it has lost four games it led in the fourth quarter, it runs for 3.6 a carry, and Indianapolis beat it 19-17 in Week 3. Mack makes it Texans −8.6, so the Colts and the points, with the Over.

Indianapolis could be missing four starters at two positions. On the Colts' first Week 11 report (Mon Nov 16, a walkthrough), CB Sauce Gardner (calf), CB Charvarius Ward (knee), LT Bernhard Raimann (concussion) and RT Jalen Travis (knee) were all DNP. So were DT Jerry Tillery (calf), TE Will Mallory (hamstring), LB Jaylon Carlies (neck) and LS Luke Rhodes (hamstring); WR Anthony Gould (toe) was limited (SI, Nov 16). Yahoo Sports adds that tackle Charlie Heck missed Tuesday for personal reasons. ESPN's depth chart (Nov 18) lists Travis as DOUBTFUL, which this page counts as out, and Raimann, Gardner and Ward as QUESTIONABLE. Its next men up are Luke Tenuta at LT, Kenneth Harris and Johnathan Edwards at corner. ESPN also lists LB Akeem Davis-Gaither, the team's leading tackler, on IR. Alec Pierce (heel) is on IR.

Houston: RB Woody Marks (concussion) is DOUBTFUL on ESPN. DeMeco Ryans called him unlikely to play, and SI says he is not expected to. DE Danielle Hunter and WR Marvin Harrison Jr. (both hamstring) were DNP Monday and are listed questionable. If Hunter sits, Jadeveon Clowney starts. LB Azeez Al-Shaair (groin), the leading tackler, is on IR. RB David Montgomery (groin) practiced fully and is expected back for the first time since Week 5 (NFL.com).

Sources: SI Texans On SI first injury report (Nov 16, fetched Nov 18); Yahoo Sports Colts–Texans preview; NFL.com "three must-know storylines"; ESPN Colts and Texans depth charts (all fetched Nov 18). Thursday's final report decides it.

Where are Daniel Jones and C.J. Stroud? their 2026 numbers against the 32 passers with the most yards, through Week 10

Answer: Jones is last, and Stroud is top ten in almost everything except completion rate. Among the league's 32 leading passers by yards, Jones ranks 32nd in passer rating, yards per attempt and interceptions, and 29th in completion rate. Stroud ranks 6th in yards, 7th in rating and tied for 3rd-fewest interceptions. NFL.com notes he is the 18th quarterback since 1982 with at least 19 touchdowns and no more than 3 interceptions through nine team games, and the only one of the 18 with a losing record at that point.

What is known instead: the Colts allow 4.8 yards a carry and 27.1 points a game. Houston's defense ranks 20th in yards allowed and 11th against the run (NFL.com), and it now plays without its leading tackler (Al-Shaair, IR). Why it matters Thursday. The Colts' route to covering is Taylor carrying 20-plus times. That depends on second-level tackling from Houston linebackers Hansen and To'oTo'o without Al-Shaair.

The gap is 3.9 points toward Indianapolis. The ratings agree Houston is clearly better, but 12.5 is a lot to lay for a team that is 3-6 against the spread and has blown four fourth-quarter leads. Six of Houston's nine games and six of Indianapolis's ten have gone over the total (Yahoo).

What would change it. If Raimann, Travis, Gardner and Ward are all ruled out Thursday, the case for the Colts gets much weaker.
