The readDetroit's offense is the best unit in this game: 32.8 points a game, Goff at 111.3 with no interceptions in five games, and Gibbs with 504 rushing yards and 7 touchdowns. Green Bay comes to Detroit without Micah Parsons (PUP, will not practice this week, per LaFleur via ESPN), Javon Hargrave and Jonathan Ford (both IR), and Edgerrin Cooper (IR). Green Bay's answer is Jordan Love throwing deep against a Detroit secondary still missing Brian Branch and Kerby Joseph (both on PUP), and a defense with 9 interceptions to Detroit's 3. Mack has Detroit winning 57% of the time but makes it Lions −2.5, so the half point goes to Green Bay at +3. On the total, every Lions game has gone over 53.5 and every Packers game has stayed under it; Mack's number sides with Green Bay's games and takes the Under.

No Wednesday practice report had been confirmed when this page was written. ESPN's Packers injury page (fetched Oct 21) lists Micah Parsons (DE, knee) on PUP-R. Its note: LaFleur said he will not practice in Week 7 and has more hurdles to clear first, so this page plans without him. Detroit's side: safeties Brian Branch (Achilles) and Kerby Joseph (knee) are both on PUP-R. On Joseph, Campbell says he "is in a good headspace" (ESPN). C Cade Mays (wrist) is on IR-R. LB Devin White and G Ben Bartch went to IR Friday, and CB Rock Ya-Sin is QUESTIONABLE. Keith Abney II and Chuck Clark start at safety.

Where is Jordan Love? Love and Goff against the other teams' leading passers, through Week 6

Answer: Love is a volume passer with average efficiency. Goff is a top-three passer who has not thrown an interception. Among the 32 teams' leading passers, Love ranks 3rd in total passing yards (1,684) but 17th in passer rating (91.8) and 27th in completion rate (59.7%). His 7.5 yards per attempt is tied for 12th, and Watson and Golden's average catch gets him there. Goff (in five games) is 3rd in rating at 111.3, behind only Lamar Jackson and Brock Purdy. He is tied for 1st in interceptions with zero, and he is 3rd in yards per game.

Missed tackles what ESPN's tackle data shows for both teams (missed-tackle counts not available)

No missed-tackle counts for 2026 were found on a page opened today, so this section uses what ESPN does publish.

Green Bay: 433 tackles and 36 tackles for loss in six games. Two of its top five tacklers are safeties (Evan Williams 52, Javon Bullard 35), a sign of how often plays get to the second level.
