The readJacksonville arrives 7-2 on a three-game win streak. It has scored 269 points and allowed 144 (ESPN schedule results), and Trevor Lawrence has 21 touchdowns against 5 interceptions with a 112.8 rating. The Giants lead the NFC East at 5-3-1, but they have been outscored 196-203 only narrowly, and J.J. McCarthy has 1 touchdown and 1 interception in three starts. The Giants' case is their defense, 5th in yards allowed (jaguars.com), and a run game that has given Cam Skattebo 5.8 yards a carry in McCarthy's two starts. Mack has Jacksonville by 6.7 against a market of 6, so the edge over the number is small. The Jaguars' injury list (Hainsey out, Tuten, Meyers and Walker limited) is the swing.

Where is J.J. McCarthy? the two starters' 2026 numbers from ESPN and the team sites, through Week 10

Answer: competent and improving, and a long way behind the quarterback across the field. McCarthy has thrown 104 passes for the Giants: 66 completions, 915 yards (8.8 a pass attempt), 2 TD, 2 INT and a 90.0 rating. As a starter he has completed 66.3% at 8.1 yards an attempt, and the Giants are 2-1 in those games. Harbaugh: "He's grown a lot in terms of knowing the offense."

Lawrence has played nine games: 2,284 yards, 21 TD, 5 INT, a 112.8 rating, and he leads the league at 13.4 yards a completion. He has been sacked 14 times, tied for 4th-fewest.

The Giants have started three quarterbacks this season: Dart (125.9 before his knee injury), Winston (79.6 in 8 games) and McCarthy.

Reassessment Mack vs. the market, and what would move it

Mack: Jaguars to win (69%), Jaguars to cover, Under 46.5. Mack's line is Giants +6.7 and the market has Jaguars −6.0. Mack is 0.7 points more bullish on Jacksonville than the market. That is a thin edge: a field goal or a key-number push decides it.

What would change it. If Tuten, Meyers or Walker is ruled out Friday, the cover case gets weaker. Monheim starting at center is already in the price. On the Giants' side, if Nic Jones misses the game, the secondary that has to handle Washington gets thinner.

The total. Jacksonville has allowed 16.0 points a game and the Giants have a top-five defense by yards, which supports the Under. The risk is Jacksonville's 29.9 points a game on offense.
