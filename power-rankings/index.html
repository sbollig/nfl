How are the ratings updated?

Todd's ratings never move on their own. After each week I shift both teams by one tenth of the surprise, the actual home margin minus the projected margin, clamped to ±21 points. The home team gains that amount and the away team loses it, so the league total never drifts. Results after Week 7: Bills, Seahawks, Bengals, Chiefs, Bears are the top five; 49ers fell 8 places, Bengals rose 9.
