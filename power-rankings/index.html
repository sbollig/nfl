How does the spreadsheet get from ratings to a spread?

Legend

Input, blue. Typed by ΖΓ559 or copied from the schedule. No formula behind it. Formula, orange. Computed by Excel from the cells above it. Column letter shown. Output, green. The one number the sheet exists to produce. Seeded, gold dashed. Started as a formula, meant to be overwritten. Rows still holding the seed have no real book line. Dead end, red dotted. Stored but referenced by nothing. Mack's addition, purple. Logic ΖΓ559 never wrote; labeled so it is never mistaken for his. −3.5Spread sign: home-side, sportsbook convention. Negative = home favored by that many points. EdgeΖΓ559's spread minus the book's. Negative = he has the home team stronger than Vegas; positive = he leans away. SUStraight up: did the team ΖΓ559's spread favored win the game. ATSAgainst the spread: did the side ΖΓ559's edge pointed to cover the book line. Blank on seeded rows. ✅ ❌Hit / miss. – means no real book line, so not scoreable. TierContender ≥ 4 · Playoff ≥ 2 · Middle −2 to 2 · Below ≥ −4 · Bottom below −4. Same cut lines used in the ranking above. ▲ ▼Move: places gained or lost versus the previous week's order. A team can move without playing when others pass it. SurpriseActual margin minus projected margin, clamped to ±21; one tenth of it moves each team, so one game is worth at most about 2 points.

How are the ratings updated?

ΖΓ559's ratings never move on their own. After each week I shift both teams by one tenth of the surprise, the actual home margin minus the projected margin, clamped to ±21 points. The home team gains that amount and the away team loses it, so the league total never drifts. Results after Week 3: Bills, Chiefs, Bears, Rams, Ravens are the top five; Packers fell 6 places, Jaguars rose 6.
