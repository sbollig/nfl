Take Todd's ratings as they stand after Week 6, run every remaining game through his spread formula, turn each projected margin into a win probability, and add them up: an expected record for all 32 teams and a projected winner in each division.

How to read a team card. The big number is the projected final record (expected wins out of 17: wins so far plus the sum of win probabilities). The line under it shows the record now and the record if every favorite wins; the gap between the two is how many coin flips the team faces. Open a card for the rating and the remaining-schedule figure.

Method. Projected home margin = home-field advantage plus the rating difference (flat 0.2 at the eight neutral-site games); results are assumed to scatter around it with a spread of 13.5 points, my parameter. A team favored by 3 wins about 59% of the time; by 10, about 77%. Ratings frozen at their post-Week-6 values; no injuries, byes or rest. Update factor one tenth of each surprise, softened from one fifth at ΖΓ636's suggestion.
