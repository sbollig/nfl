How to read a card. Lines are home-side, book sign: a negative number means the home team is favored by that much. Mack is his line after this week's verified injury hits. Edge is his line minus the market; he takes the side his line favors, and the further the edge is from zero the stronger the opinion. The win % is his straight-up confidence.

Key injuries are the top three per team from the ESPN injury report (Out, IR, Doubtful, Questionable); the arrow shows the next healthy player at that spot on the ESPN depth chart. "Depth piece" means the injured player was not a starter. Players hurt in last week's games are included. Fetched Oct 18.

QB notes applied. Ravens: Jackson out, Tyler Huntley starts. Eagles: Hurts out, Tanner McKee starts. Buccaneers: Mayfield out, Jalon Daniels starts. Giants: Dart out, Jameis Winston starts.

Card colors. Before a game, a card wears the colors of the team Mack picked to win. Once the final is in, it switches to the colors of the team that actually won. The Broncos game stays in Broncos colours either way.

Picks lock at kickoff (the Sunday 10 AM games lock at 10:15 AM Pacific). You can change a pick any time before then. After that, everyone's picks for that game are read-only; the scores can still be entered.

Points. One point for each correct pick: winner, spread, and over/under, so up to three a game. A push or a blank pick scores nothing. Mack's picks sit in the row under yours on each card. Like everyone's, they can change until that game kicks off and not after. The tiles at the top show each person's total, with the three records underneath. Mack is scored the same way on the picks he made: a winner in every game, a spread pick in every game (the side his line favors against the market; where the two are equal, the side of his winner), and an over/under in every game. His totals come from each team's points scored and allowed so far this season, with a deduction for a missing starting quarterback.
