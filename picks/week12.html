How these two boards work

Season standings adds up every point each of us has earned this season, scored exactly like the weekly board: one point each for the right winner, the right side of the spread, and the right over/under (the Broncos props game counts the same way). A blank pick or a push scores nothing. Mack's picks are graded the same way as everyone else's.

Made picks only, missed thrown out is the even-footing view. It counts only the picks all four of us made. If anyone left a pick blank, whether a winner, a spread or an over/under, that pick is thrown out for everyone, so nobody gains or loses ground from a skipped pick. That is why its totals are lower, and why the order can differ from the season standings.

How to read a card. Lines are home-side, book sign: a negative number means the home team is favored by that much. Mack is his line after this week's verified injury hits. Edge is his line minus the market; he takes the side his line favors, and the further the edge is from zero the stronger the opinion. The win % is his straight-up confidence.

Key injuries are the top three per team from the ESPN injury report (Out, IR, Doubtful, Questionable); the arrow shows the next healthy player at that spot on the ESPN depth chart. "Depth piece" means the injured player was not a starter. Players hurt in last week's games are included. Fetched Nov 24.

Card colors. Before a game, a card wears the colors of the team Mack picked to win. Once the final is in, it switches to the colors of the team that actually won. The Broncos game stays in Broncos colours either way.

Picks lock at kickoff (the Sunday 10 AM games lock at 10:15 AM Pacific). You can change a pick any time before then. After that, everyone's picks for that game are read-only; the scores can still be entered.

Points. One point for each correct pick: winner, spread, and over/under, so up to three a game. A push or a blank pick scores nothing. Mack's picks sit in the row under yours on each card. Like everyone's, they can change until that game kicks off and not after. The tiles at the top show each person's total, with the three records underneath. Mack is scored the same way on the picks he made: a winner in every game, a spread pick in every game (the side his line favors against the market; where the two are equal, the side of his winner), and an over/under in every game.
