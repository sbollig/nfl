No Week 8 practice report yet on either team site as of Wednesday morning (denverbroncos.com and chiefs.com news pages, fetched Oct 28; the last Chiefs report is dated Oct 23). Allen hurt the hamstring Sunday (TheDNVR via ESPN, Oct 25). Under the house rule a questionable starter is planned as a coin flip until Friday; if Surtain and Hufanga sit again it is Jahdae Barron outside, Devon Key or Taylor Rapp at strong safety and Sai'vion Jones taking Allen's snaps (ESPN depth chart, Oct 28).

Travis Kelce (ankle) and L'Jarius Sneed (knee) were limited all last week but played. LB Nick Bolton left Sunday's game briefly with neck spasms and returned (chiefs.com, Oct 26). The Chiefs signed kicker Justin Tucker to the practice squad on Oct 28 (chiefs.com transactions) after Harrison Butker missed from 58 and 45 in Seattle.

Where is Bo Nix? (and where is Mahomes?) 2026 numbers against the other 31 teams' leading passers, through Week 7

Answer: still bottom quarter as a passer, top ten at staying clean and at protecting the ball, and Sunday was his best game. Against the 32 teams' leading passers, Nix ranks 26th in passer rating, 29th in completion rate and 29th in yards per attempt (5.8). He is 9th-lowest in interception rate and 6th-lowest in sack rate. Arizona was the first time this year he looked like a Payton quarterback: 222 yards through the air, a 52-yard touchdown to Harvey and a 62-yard touchdown run, the first Broncos game with a 50-yard rushing score and a 50-yard passing score since 2002 (denverbroncos.com, Oct 25). His own read: "I thought we played a good game offensively." Payton: "The confidence inside the building, relative to his abilities, hasn't changed at all." Mahomes, for comparison, is 14th in rating (95.3), 10th in yards per attempt (7.7) and 3rd-lowest in sack rate, and has gone two games without a touchdown pass.

Coaching staffs tenure, experience, and what the beat has said this season

Broncos

  • Sean Payton, head coach since 2023; 37–21 in Denver, 189–110 regular season and 10–10 in the playoffs for his career (Wikipedia, Oct 28; Denver record includes Sunday's win). He has called the plays himself since Week 5; Denver is 3-0 since at 23.3 points a game (Covers, Oct 28). After Arizona: "It was one of our more complete games." (denverbroncos.com, Oct 25)
  • Davis Webb, offensive coordinator; Zach Strief, run game coordinator and assistant head coach; John Morton, pass game coordinator.
  • Vance Joseph, defensive coordinator since 2023; Robert Livingston, defensive pass game coordinator.
  • Darren Rizzi, special teams coordinator and assistant head coach. Logan Kilgore coaches the quarterbacks.

Chiefs

  • Andy Reid, head coach since January 2013; 154–65 in Kansas City, 284–158–1 regular season and 28–17 in the playoffs for his career, three Super Bowl wins (Wikipedia, Oct 28). After Seattle: "All three phases, we struggled. We have to do better, and that's my responsibility." (chiefs.com, Oct 26)
  • Eric Bieniemy, offensive coordinator, back on a multi-year deal signed Jan 23, 2026; Chiefs OC 2018–22, Commanders OC 2023, UCLA OC 2024, Bears running backs coach 2025 (Wikipedia, Oct 28).
  • Steve Spagnuolo, defensive coordinator since January 2019, age 66; former Rams head coach (10–38).
  • Dave Toub, assistant head coach and special teams coordinator, in Kansas City since 2013 after nine years running the Bears' units. David Girardi coaches the quarterbacks.

Missed tackles Denver's number since Week 1, and Kansas City's

Denver: 56 missed tackles, the most in the NFL; Kansas City: 26, tied for 27th with Arizona (Pro Football Reference, via BetMGM, published Oct 28, so through Week 7). The Lions have the fewest at 12. Denver's count has gone from 44 (through Week 5) to 54 (through Week 6) to 56, so Arizona cost only two missed tackles, the best tackling game of the year.

Why it matters Sunday. This is the one number that explains Week 1: Walker's 173 yards came on 23 carries with a 60-yard run, and Kansas City leads the league at 5.3 yards a carry as a team with seven runs of 20 or more. Denver allows 4.6 a carry. Seattle held Walker to 36 yards on 14 carries by not missing, and Kansas City scored 3 points. Alex Singleton (87 tackles, the week's AFC Defensive Player) and Justin Strnad have to get him down on first contact.

The other direction. Kansas City allows 3.6 a carry and misses half as many tackles. Denver's explosives Sunday (Harvey's 52-yard screen, Nix's 62-yard run) were yards after a broken tackle or a missed fit; that will be rarer against this front.

Reassessment Mack's line vs. the market, and the three props

Where Mack and the market stand (Wednesday). Mack's rating line is Broncos −1.2 and his straight-up pick is Denver at 54%; the market, frozen at the board build, is Chiefs −1.5 and a total of 41.5. That is a 2.7-point disagreement and a two-sided one: Mack has Denver as the better team on the ratings plus home field, and the market still has Kansas City as the better team. ESPN's Matchup Predictor (Oct 28) sides with the market at 55.7% Chiefs, and ESPN's live DraftKings total had already drifted to 40.5 from an opener of 43.5.

The props. This week the board asks for Over/Under on total sacks, total takeaways and total points instead of winner and spread. The inputs: Denver's defense averages 3.0 sacks a game and Kansas City's line allows 1.2; Kansas City's defense averages 1.3 and Denver's line allows 1.3. The Week 1 game had 6 sacks (4 on Nix, 2 on Mahomes), 3 takeaways and 41 points. Season takeaway rates are 1.7 a game for Kansas City (10 in six) and 1.0 a game in interceptions alone for Denver (7 in seven, plus fumble recoveries ESPN's team page does not label cleanly). Points: Denver scores 20.6 and allows 20.1; Kansas City scores 23.8 and allows 17.2. Mack's judgment, not the formula: sacks lean Over on Denver's pass rush against two backup tackles, takeaways lean Under because both quarterbacks are top-ten in interception rate, and points lean Under with the house pick.

What would change it. Surtain and Hufanga active moves Denver's defensive rating back to its Week 3 level and firms up the pick; Surtain, Hufanga and Allen all out costs −0.45 under the formula and makes Mack's line roughly a pick'em. Rice back for Kansas City adds its only outside receiver with a touchdown. Moore cleared from the concussion protocol takes the sacks lean back toward the number.
