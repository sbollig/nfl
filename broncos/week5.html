House rule: doubtful means out. Pat Surtain II is listed DOUBTFUL (Payton, Wednesday: does not expect him to play), so this page treats him as out. Pat Bryant (WR, doubtful, ankle) is treated as out too. Riley Moss is QUESTIONABLE with a rib injury (imaging Monday). The next men up are Jahdae Barron (also the backup nickel) and Kris Abrams-Draine, with Ja'Quan McMillian in the slot.

Denver starts Jahdae Barron at left corner with Riley Moss (questionable, ribs) on the right; if Moss cannot go, Kris Abrams-Draine starts and the nickel thins out. The Chargers' two leading receivers, Tre' Harris (13 catches, 209 yards, 16.1 per catch) and Ladd McConkey (12 for 183, 15.3 per catch), are exactly the kind of deep-ball targets a backup pair struggles with. With Surtain out, the pass-coverage row in the unit table below moves to the Chargers, and the board's number moves from Broncos −3.5 (60%) to Broncos −3.1 (59%); see the reassessment below.

Missed tackles the number Denver's defense has carried since Week 1

Broncos defense — most missed tackles in the NFL

  • Season: 44 missed tackles through Week 4, most in the league (Eagles next at 36), per Pro Football Reference; PFF counts 48. Rush defense 142.8 yards a game, 30th.
  • Week 1 at Kansas City: 28 missed tackles, a 29.8% miss rate, 247 yards gained after missed tackles (Next Gen Stats); the third-worst single game since 2018. PFF counted 24.
  • Week 2 vs Jacksonville: 10 missed tackles. Weeks 3 and 4 per-game counts have not been published; in Week 4 Hufanga alone missed four (PFF).
  • Who is missing them (PFF, through Week 4): Talanoa Hufanga 8 · Riley Moss 6 · Ja'Quan McMillian 6 · Alex Singleton 6. Through Week 2, Moss's miss rate was 40%.

Why it matters Sunday. Three of the four leaders are the secondary (Hufanga, Moss, McMillian), and Moss is questionable. The Chargers' Omarion Hampton has forced 15 missed tackles on 59 carries with 3.12 yards after contact per attempt (PFF). A tackling-poor defense against a contact-heavy back is the Chargers' path to controlling the game.

Sources: PFR totals via BetMGM, Oct 7 · PFF counts, Yahoo Oct 6 · Week 1 NGS, Denver7 · Week 4 PFF grades · Hampton, PFF

Chargers defense

  • Season: 24 missed tackles through Week 4, 10th-most (Pro Football Reference). Per-game counts and a Next Gen Stats miss rate have not been published.
  • Week 4 at Seattle: three sacks; Tuipulotu had an interception and a forced fumble.

Denver's counter. J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey are averaging 3.6 a carry behind a line that has not produced a 20-yard run. Denver is more likely to beat missed tackles with Harvey's 20 catches out of the backfield than with the ground game.

Reassessment doubtful players treated as out

What changed. Surtain (CB) and Bryant (WR) move from doubtful to out. Under the board's injury rule that takes Denver's one-week hit from −1.4 to −1.8; the Chargers' hit stays −2.35 (Slater out, eleven questionable). Nobody on the Chargers is doubtful.

Mack's view (judgment, not the formula). The rule prices a cornerback at half a point, the same as a fullback. Losing Surtain against this pair of receivers is worth more than that, and the tackling numbers put three of Denver's four worst tacklers in the same secondary. I keep the Broncos straight up on the strength of the pass-rush mismatch and Herbert's six interceptions, but I would not lay 3.5 points. If a side has to be taken against the spread, it is the Chargers +3.5 at home.

Formula: HFA + (rating + injury hit) difference, rating scale 13.5 points per standard deviation; ratings after Week 4 (Broncos +2.7, Chargers −1.0, Chargers HFA 1.2). Rule: non-QB Out −0.5, Doubtful −0.3, Questionable −0.15, capped at −2.5.
