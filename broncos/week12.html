J.K. Dobbins (RB) is OUT: the Broncos suspended him one game for "conduct detrimental to the team." Payton gave no reason, saying "it's better that way" (CBS Sports, fetched Nov 24). Dobbins leads the team with 99 carries but played one snap against the Raiders. RJ Harvey (RB, knee) is on IR and cannot return until Week 13, so Jonah Coleman carries the load with Cody Schrader behind him. Tyler Badie (RB, thumb) was a full participant but must still be activated from IR.

Tuesday's estimates, Broncos (walkthrough): DE Sai'vion Jones (hamstring) did not participate, QUESTIONABLE on ESPN; S Taylor Rapp (quad) limited, QUESTIONABLE; S Brandon Jones (hamstring), WR Pat Bryant (ankle) and S JL Skinner (shoulder) full, as was TE Caleb Lohner (foot, IR, must be activated). Brandon Jones missed the last two games; this was his first full listing since a Week 8 injury. OLB Jonah Elliss (ankle) remains on IR.

Tuesday's estimates, Steelers (walkthrough): OLB Alex Highsmith (ankle) did not participate and ESPN lists him DOUBTFUL; under the house rule doubtful means out. OLB Nick Herbig (hip/ribs) was limited, QUESTIONABLE. WR Ben Skowronek (calf) did not participate, QUESTIONABLE. WR Michael Pittman Jr. (foot) and G Gennings Dunker (knee) were full but are still on IR, designated for return. McCarthy is "very confident" Pittman plays (Yahoo Sports); he wants better pass protection from Dunker first (BVM Sports). If both edge rushers sit, Jack Sawyer and KJ Henry cover.

Missed tackles missed tackles, and the total tackles-for-loss prop

Tackles for loss: two very different games to average. Summed from ESPN box scores, Denver's ten games have produced 7.4 combined tackles for loss and the Steelers' ten have produced 11.1. Denver's defense has 53 and its offense has allowed only 21; the Steelers' defense has 59 and their offense has allowed 52. ESPN's season pages credit slightly more (57 for Denver's defense, 63 for the Steelers').

  • Broncos games, combined: 6, 7, 10, 1, 14, 8, 7, 5, 10, 6
  • Steelers games, combined: 11, 14, 6, 11, 6, 18, 9, 14, 7, 15
  • Steelers at Acrisure: 11, 6, 6, 14

How to use it. Denver's share of the number should come against a line that has allowed 5.2 a game. The Steelers' share runs into an offense that gives up 2.1. Watt (11) leads both teams; Highsmith (7, doubtful) and Herbig (7, questionable) are 14 of the Steelers' season count, so their status pulls the number down. Judgment, not a formula: I would expect a result between the two averages, and I have no confirmed prop number to set it against.

Missed tackles. SumerSports had Denver on pace for a 16.2% missed-tackle rate, its highest since at least 2016 (Athlon via Yahoo Sports, Nov 21). The Steelers had only four in Philadelphia per PFF, with Payton Wilson at 76.2 and Jamel Dean at 80.0 in tackling (steelcurtainnetwork.com). I could not open a league-wide team count this week, so none is given.

Reassessment Mack's frozen pick against the market, and what would change it

Where it stands. Mack's pick was frozen at the board build and has not been changed here.

  • Line: Mack Steelers +1.9; market Broncos −3.0
  • Winner: Mack Broncos, 56%; ESPN Matchup Predictor Broncos 61.3%
  • Spread side: Mack Steelers, because 3.0 is 1.1 more than the ratings ask
  • Total: Mack Over; market 38.5 at the freeze, 37.5 in the Nov 24 feed

Total points. Denver's games average 44.6 points and seven of ten went past 38.5. The Steelers' average 41.5, with five of ten past it, and their four home games 44.8. The market has moved the other way on weather: the Denver Gazette reports an 80% chance of precipitation and a high of 44, possibly a rain and sleet mix; Covers has about 34 degrees at game time with winds of 13–14 mph. The Steelers scored 34 total in their last two games, and Denver has averaged 30.5 over its last four.

First Broncos TD scorer. Denver's first touchdown of each game, in order: Engram, Adkins, Bryant, Waddle, Dobbins, Dobbins, Nix, Waddle, Sutton, Trautman. Seven of ten were passes. Coleman has not had one, but he has six touchdowns in six games with a box-score line and the other two backs to score a rushing touchdown, Dobbins and Harvey, are unavailable. BetMGM's anytime prices (Nov 24, not first-TD): Coleman −105, Waddle +260, Sutton +390, Bryant +450, Engram +475, Nix +500, Mims +600, Trautman +1050. My lean is Coleman, with Waddle the alternative if you expect Denver to score through the air first.

What would change it. Herbig ruled out with Highsmith would strengthen the Broncos side. Both edge rushers playing, or Brandon Jones missing again, would help the Steelers. Heavy rain at kickoff is the case against the Over. Wednesday's reports and Thursday's statuses come first.
